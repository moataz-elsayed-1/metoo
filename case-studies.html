🎯 Objective

Generate real WhatsApp conversations that lead to actual sales for a luxury car page. The primary goal was not just traffic — it was qualified buyers ready to talk.

🔎 Strategy

  • Campaign segmentation based on car models (BMW – Range Rover – and others)
  • Targeting cold audiences with interests in luxury cars and strong purchasing behavior
  • Retargeting warm audiences: engaged users and page visitors
  • Daily performance optimization — pausing high-cost ads immediately
  • Gradual scaling of the best-performing campaigns

📈 Results

640 WhatsApp Conversations Reach: 204,532 Impressions: 447,670 Avg. CPL: 18.21 EGP Total Spend: 16,655.85 EGP Best CPL: 10.89 EGP

💡 What Made the Difference?

We weren't just selling cars — we were selling trust, luxury, and exclusivity. Optimization was continuous: pausing the highest-cost ads, increasing budgets for top performers, and constant testing of creatives and copy.

📌 Conclusion: With a budget of around 18K EGP, we generated 640 highly interested potential buyers. Some campaigns achieved a cost per conversation as low as 10.89 EGP — a very strong number in the high-ticket luxury car market. This proves that the right strategy always beats a bigger budget.

🎯 Objective

Generate qualified WhatsApp conversations that convert into real sales for a luxury car brand. Focus on high-ticket models: GLC200, GLC300 Coupe, E200, and BMW.

🔎 Strategy

  • Campaign segmentation based on car models (GLC200 – GLC300 Coupe – E200 – BMW)
  • Targeting users interested in luxury cars with strong purchasing behavior
  • Retargeting engaged users and page visitors to maximize conversion
  • Daily optimization to progressively reduce cost per conversation
  • Scaling the highest-performing campaigns aggressively

📈 Results

536 WhatsApp Conversations Reach: 125,305 Impressions: 247,800 Avg. CPL: 14.01 EGP Total Spend: 7,508.47 EGP Best CPL: 8.72 EGP

💡 Key Success Factor

We didn't rely on a single ad. We consistently tested multiple creatives and copy variations while optimizing performance on a daily basis. The combination of precise audience segmentation and relentless creative testing was the key.

📌 Conclusion: With a budget of around 7,500 EGP, we generated 536 highly qualified potential buyers. This proves that the right strategy and proper audience segmentation make a bigger difference than anything else — even in a highly competitive, high-ticket market.
