✦ About Me

Performance Over Everything

I help brands scale through structured, data-driven paid advertising systems — not random campaigns.

As a Media Buyer in Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram), I focus on building full-funnel strategies that turn attention into measurable revenue. My approach is analytical, testing-based, and ROI-oriented.

My core strengths include Full Funnel Strategy (TOF → MOF → BOF), campaign structuring and scaling frameworks, creative testing systems, and budget optimization based strictly on performance metrics. Deep focus on ROAS, CAC, CTR, and Conversion Rate.

With a background in Management Information Systems (MIS), I combine marketing strategy with technical logic. I specialize in Full Stack Web Development — building websites, funnels, and tracking systems that power high-performing campaigns.
